How this works

NotMyRecruiter is a community-maintained accountability record for recruiter conduct. It exists because candidates invest significant time, energy, and professional credibility in the interview process — and when recruiters go silent after that investment, there is currently no accountability mechanism. This platform is not a place to vent. It is a factual record of professional conduct.

What this platform is

Recruiters are professionals acting in a professional capacity. When a candidate completes an interview, the minimum standard of professional courtesy is a response — even a brief one. This platform documents cases where that standard was not met. Every submission is anonymous. Every recruiter is identified by their public LinkedIn profile only.

What qualifies as a reportable incident

You completed one or more interviews and received no follow-up after 14 or more days. A recruiter who initiated contact with you went silent after you responded. A recruiter cancelled a scheduled interview and never rescheduled or acknowledged your follow-up. You received only an automated rejection with no human follow-up after a final-round interview.

What does not qualify

A recruiter declining to provide feedback on your application or interview performance. A rejection delivered promptly and professionally, even if disappointing. Any conduct that occurred less than 14 days ago. Anything that cannot be characterized as professional conduct — personal grievances, compensation disputes, or matters unrelated to recruiter communication.
