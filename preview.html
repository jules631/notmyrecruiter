How this works

NotMyRecruiter is a community-maintained accountability record for recruiter conduct. It exists because candidates invest significant time, energy, and professional credibility in the interview process — and when recruiters go silent after that investment, there is currently no accountability mechanism. This platform is not a place to vent. It is a factual record of professional conduct, maintained to help candidates make informed decisions.

What this platform is

Recruiters are professionals acting in a professional capacity. When a candidate completes an interview, the minimum standard of professional courtesy is a response — even a brief one. This platform documents cases where that standard was not met. Every submission is anonymous. Every recruiter is identified by their public LinkedIn profile only — a self-published professional record they have chosen to make public. No personal information beyond professional identity is collected or displayed.

What qualifies as a reportable incident

All four of the following qualify: 1. You completed one or more interviews and received no follow-up — not even a form rejection — after 14 or more days. 2. A recruiter who initiated contact with you went silent after you responded. 3. A recruiter cancelled a scheduled interview and never rescheduled, acknowledged your follow-up, or provided any response. 4. You received only an automated rejection with no human follow-up after a final-round or late-stage interview.

What does not qualify

The following are explicitly not reportable on this platform: - A recruiter declining to provide feedback on your application or interview performance. This is legally protected conduct and is not a professional failing. - A rejection delivered promptly and professionally, even if disappointing. - Any conduct that occurred less than 14 days ago. Time-gating exists to prevent premature submissions and reduce false reports. - Anything that cannot be characterized as professional conduct — personal grievances, compensation disputes, or matters unrelated to recruiter communication.

How submissions are verified

Verification exists in three tiers, each conferring a different trust signal displayed on the report: Tier 1 — Email verified: Your submission email is verified before the report is published. Your email is never stored in plaintext and is never displayed publicly. Tier 2 — Header verified: You have provided the From: and Date: fields of a relevant email header confirming the recruiter's domain and the date of contact. This is the strongest submitter-side verification available. Tier 3 — Community verified: A report that has remained published for 90 or more days without being successfully disputed or reaching the flag threshold earns a community verified status. All submissions are also subject to a 14-day gate — no report can be published until at least 14 days have elapsed since the reported incident date. Submitters are limited to one report per company per 90-day window.

How disputes work

Recruiters and companies named in a report have three paths to dispute it: Tier 1 — Recruiter self-identifies: The recruiter submits a dispute from an email address matching their company domain, and provides their LinkedIn URL matching the one on the report. If standing is verified, they may publish a rebuttal statement of up to 500 characters that appears publicly alongside the report. The report itself remains published. Tier 2 — Factual inaccuracy claim: The recruiter provides Tier 1 standing plus email header evidence demonstrating that contact was made. This triggers an admin review. The outcome — report annotated, modified, or removed — is determined solely by the admin. No automated removal path exists. Tier 3 — Legal or formal request: A written request submitted to the LLC's registered agent. This is the only dispute path available to HR or legal teams acting on behalf of a company without recruiter identity verification. Reviewed by admin and counsel as appropriate. The following are not valid grounds for removal: - A company claiming reputational harm - Disagreement with how an experience was characterized - Volume of dispute requests from the same company domain - A dispute submitted from a personal or non-company email address

Community flagging

Any visitor may flag a report as inaccurate. A report that reaches five flags is automatically placed under review and hidden from the public feed pending admin evaluation. Flags are deduplicated by hashed IP address — one flag per report per visitor. Flagging a report because you disagree with it, or because you represent the company named, is not a valid use of the flag system. Abuse of the flag system may result in submissions from your network being deprioritized.

Prohibited content

Submissions that contain any of the following will be removed and the submitter account banned: - Personal attacks, insults, or language targeting the recruiter as a person rather than documenting their professional conduct - Identifying information beyond professional identity — home address, personal email, phone number, or any non-public personal detail - False or fabricated accounts of events - Content submitted on behalf of another person without their knowledge - Harassment of any kind

Legal and platform structure

NotMyRecruiter operates as an LLC. As a platform for user-generated content, it is protected under Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act, which provides federal immunity to interactive computer services from liability for content submitted by users. Submitters are solely responsible for the accuracy of their submissions. By submitting a report, you attest that the account is truthful, based on your direct personal experience, and does not contain prohibited content as defined above.
